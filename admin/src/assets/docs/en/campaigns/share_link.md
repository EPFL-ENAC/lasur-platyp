**Share the link to the questionnaire**

Once you have entered the campaign settings and selected the employee mobility support measures, you can share the link to the campaign-specific questionnaire by clicking on the "Survey link" button.

**Sample email template to facilitate sharing**

To make it easier for participants to share the questionnaire and understand the process, we are providing an ‘email template’ that outlines the campaign’s objectives and explains why and how to take part. By entering the contact email address and the name of the person in charge of the initiative in the fields provided, the template email will update automatically.

However, we recommend adapting this template to personalise your communication about the campaign and thus tailor Mobilyse to your organisation.
