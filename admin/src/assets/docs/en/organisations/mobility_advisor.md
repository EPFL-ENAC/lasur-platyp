## Mobility Advisor Role

Some people may need access to the key findings of the mobility assessment, without, however, being involved in campaign management or organisational administration, nor having access to disaggregated data. We have created a specific role for these needs: the Mobility Advisor role.

### Adding a Mobility Advisor

You can add a Mobility Advisor when creating or editing the organisation by entering the relevant person’s email address. When they create their account, they will be directly assigned to the organisation as a Mobility Advisor.

### Rights of a mobility advisor

A mobility advisor can access the aggregated results of the company’s campaigns (the entire Dashboard) but cannot download the disaggregated campaign data, which is reserved for the organisation’s administrators.
They are also unable to create a campaign, or to directly edit a campaign or the organisation.
