## Mobility Advisor Role

Some people may need access to the key findings of the mobility assessment, without being directly involved in managing campaigns or the organisation. We have created a specific role for these needs: the Mobility Advisor role.

### Adding a Mobility Advisor

You can add a Mobility Advisor when creating or editing the organisation by entering the relevant person’s email address. When they create their account, they will be automatically assigned to the organisation as a Mobility Advisor.

### Rights of a mobility advisor

A mobility advisor can access the aggregated results of the company’s campaigns (the entire Dashboard). This person can also download the disaggregated data (which remains anonymous) to carry out further analysis and thus obtain more detail than the Dashboard provides.
However, they cannot create a campaign, nor can they directly edit a campaign or the organisation: only administrators can make direct changes to the organisation’s and campaigns’ data.
