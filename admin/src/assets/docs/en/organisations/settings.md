## Organisation Settings

### Administrators

Administrators are users who have full access to manage the organisation's mobility campaigns, participants, and data.

**How to add administrators:**

- Enter the email address of each administrator in the administrators field
- Press Enter after each email to add it to the list
- Multiple administrators can be assigned to a single organisation
- Administrators can view and modify all organisation campaigns and participant data

**Purpose:**

- Enables decentralized management of mobility campaigns
- Allows organisation representatives to manage their own mobility initiatives
- Provides secure access control to organisation-specific data

### Contact Information

Contact information helps participants and system users know who to reach out to for mobility-related questions.

**Contact Name:**

- The name of the person responsible for mobility coordination within the organisation
- This information may be shared with campaign participants

**Contact Email:**

- Must be a valid email address
- Used for mobility-related communications
- Can be displayed to participants in campaigns

**Information URL:**

- Provides a link to additional organisation mobility resources
- Could link to:
  - Internal mobility policies
  - Sustainable transport initiatives
  - Employee transportation benefits
  - Organisation-specific mobility guidelines

Note: Personal information entered into the Mobilyse tool is processed in accordance with our [privacy policy](https://modus-ge.ch/mobilyse-notice-protection-des-donnees).
