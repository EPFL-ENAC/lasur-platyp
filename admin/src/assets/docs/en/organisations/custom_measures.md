## Custom Measures

Companies may have unique mobility initiatives that aren't covered by the standard measures. Custom measures allow you to define organisation-specific actions.

### Creating Custom Measures

1. Click the "Custom measures" button when editing a organisation
2. Select the appropriate group for your custom measure:

   - Global measures
   - Public transport measures
   - Train measures
   - Inter-modality measures
   - Bicycle measures
   - Carpooling measures
   - Electric vehicle measures
   - Professional measures (various categories)

3. Provide labels in both English and French
4. Click "Add" to create the custom measure

### Using Custom Measures

Once created, custom measures appear alongside standard measures in the employer measures selection dropdowns. They can be selected for the organisation or for specific campaigns.

**Use cases for custom measures:**

- Organisation-specific benefits not covered by standard options
- Pilot programs or temporary initiatives
- Location-specific measures (e.g., organisation-run ferry service)
- Specialized equipment or facilities
