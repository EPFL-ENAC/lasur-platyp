### What are the next steps for your mobility strategy?
Once an initial mobility assessment has been carried out with Mobilyse, you will have an overview of the current state of your employees’ commuting and work-related mobility. This includes, in particular, the modes of transport currently used, the distances travelled, mobility equipment and constraints, as well as the carbon and health impacts of participants’ mobility.
You also have, via the second section of the Dashboard, an overview of potential changes that could be made in light of employees’ situations, preferences and constraints.
This allows you to proceed with confidence, if you wish, with the corporate mobility plan process, which comprises the following steps:

1. Appoint a mobility manager for the company
2. Identify any need for support* …
3. … for the implementation of mobility measures as part of a mobility plan**
4. Reassess periodically and/or in line with the life cycle of your organisation to measure changes in employees’ mobility practices and thus the impact of the support measures. In this way, in line with accessibility, transport modes and societal changes, the mobility measures you propose can evolve to remain relevant at all times.
