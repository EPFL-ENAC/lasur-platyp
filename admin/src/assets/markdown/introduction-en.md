#### How to read the dashboard?

This dashboard contains the results of the campaigns you have managed, organised into three sections.
The first section, 'Mobility analysis', provides information on current mobility practices and their impacts (for example, the modal share of car use for commuting is XX %, according to the 'Modal split' graph).
The second section, 'Mobility potentials', shows which modes of transport have been recommended to participants, and what the impact would be (on CO₂ emissions, health, modal shares, etc.) if participants were to adopt these recommendations (for example, the car modal share would be YY % according to the 'Potential modal split' graph).
Finally, the third section, 'Encouraging behavioural changes', provides information on changes in participants' mobility behaviour (for example, ZZ % of participants are motivated to adopt the recommendation suggested to them, as shown in the 'Motivation to adopt recommendations' chart).
Important: The results displayed here relate only to the NN participants who completed the questionnaire. You can calculate the impacts for all employees by extrapolating these results across your entire workforce.

#### Glossary

Here we explain the meanings of the acronyms used and what the modes of transport shown entail.

**Charts on "Home-to-work commute"**

• Walking: Includes any journey walked for more than 10 minutes
• Electric bicycle: Includes electric bikes as well as cargo bikes, longtail bikes, etc.
• PT: Public transports, includes buses, trams, the underground, ferries and trains
• Other: Includes micromobility (skateboards, scooters, rollerblades, etc.)
• IMT: Individual Motorised Transport, includes cars (including car-sharing), motorbikes and scooters
• AM: Active Mobility, includes walking and all types of bicycles, as well as micromobility ("other")
• PT + bicycle: includes all mode combinations that involve public transport and cycling in all its forms (mechanical, electric, cargo).
• PT + AM: includes any combination involving public transport and active modes
• TIM + Public transport: includes any combination involving private motorised transport and public transport
• TIM + Active Mobility: includes any combination involving private motorised transport and active modes

**Charts on "Professional travel"**

• Bicycle (or electric bike): groups together mechanical and electric bicycles but does not include cargo bikes
• Boat: May refer to river services (short distance) as well as ferries (long distance)
